# CampusFlow City — Fund My Crazy 2026 Submission Pack

## Recommended category
**Our Education**

Fund My Crazy asks participants to reimagine one element of the city. CampusFlow City reimagines the **education ecosystem**: instead of students navigating disconnected universities, courses, scholarships, exams, documents and deadlines, the city becomes one connected education network built around the learner.

## One-line idea
**Turn the city into a connected education system where every student can discover a path, understand their fit and know the next step.**

## Core pitch
A student's education journey is fragmented across websites, forms, PDFs, spreadsheets, emails and deadlines. CampusFlow City creates an intelligent student command center that connects those pieces into one journey.

Students build an Education Passport, discover universities and opportunities, compare paths, manage applications and documents, track deadlines and use Gemini-powered guidance to decide what to do next.

The bigger idea is not another admissions dashboard. It is a **city-scale education layer**: a future where the education resources of a city are discoverable, understandable and actionable through one student-centered experience.

## Why this matters
Education systems are often organized around institutions, while students experience them as a maze. The cost is not only inconvenience: missed deadlines, poor information, confusing choices and lost opportunities can change a student's future.

CampusFlow City shifts the experience from **searching and reacting** to **understanding and acting**.

## What makes the vision different
- **Education Passport:** one profile for goals, academics, interests, budget and preferences.
- **Education City:** one connected discovery layer for universities, courses, scholarships, exams and opportunities.
- **Gemini guidance:** turns complex information into personalized explanations, comparisons and next-best actions.
- **Application command center:** connects documents, deadlines and application progress instead of leaving students to manage them separately.
- **Pathway simulation:** helps students explore alternative education routes before committing to one.

## Future vision
CampusFlow City can evolve from an individual student tool into shared city infrastructure for education: institutions, scholarship providers, mentors, public programs and students connected through a common, intelligent discovery layer.

The long-term goal is a city where a student can ask one question — **"What should I do next?"** — and receive a useful, explainable path forward.

## Gemini role
Gemini is central to the experience, not a decorative chatbot. It can help students reason over their profile and application context, explain opportunities, summarize documents, compare pathways, identify next actions and turn complex education information into understandable decisions.

For the Fund My Crazy visual, use Google Gemini image generation to visualize the city-wide education ecosystem rather than a generic laptop dashboard.

## Suggested Gemini visual prompt
Create a cinematic near-future Indian city where education is visible as a connected civic infrastructure. Show diverse college students moving through a vibrant city while transparent digital pathways connect universities, colleges, libraries, scholarships, exams, mentors, laboratories, internships and public learning spaces. In the center, show an intelligent personal education passport creating a clear glowing path from a student's goals to opportunities and next actions. The visual should feel optimistic, human, inclusive and realistic, not like a sci-fi spaceship or corporate dashboard. Use subtle Indian urban details, modern campuses, public transport, libraries and community spaces. Make the idea immediately understandable as **a city redesigned around education and student opportunity**. No readable brand names or fake statistics.

## Scoring alignment
### Vision — 30%
Reframes education as connected city infrastructure rather than a collection of isolated institutions and websites.

### Real-life relevance — 20%
Targets everyday student problems: information overload, fragmented applications, deadlines, documents and confusing choices.

### Built with Gemini — 20%
Gemini is used for personalized guidance, explanation, document understanding, pathway reasoning and next-best-action support; the competition visual should also be generated with Google Gemini.

### Future focused — 15%
Creates a foundation for a city-wide education layer connecting students with the resources and opportunities around them.

### Execution — 15%
The concept is grounded in a working product direction with a React/Vite interface, Firebase authentication/data services and Gemini-powered application workflows.
